## Event Video: [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jWjOIfMA9qc)

### Event Objectives

- Discuss the future of cloud computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Vietnam.
- Gain strategic insights into engineering at scale from AWS technology leadership.
- Explore trends in application development, system architecture, and the evolving role of developers in the AI era.

### Speaker

- **Dr. Werner Vogels**: Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of Amazon, with extensive experience in cloud computing and distributed systems.

### Key Highlights

#### The Origins of AWS

- Review the origins and evolution of Amazon Web Services (AWS).
- Explore the foundational thinking behind building cloud services that serve customers worldwide, from small to large scale.

#### Engineering at Scale

- Examine the challenges of designing and operating systems with large numbers of users and processing requests.
- Understand the importance of reliability, automation, and scalability in system architecture.

#### The Renaissance Developer

- Explore the changing role of software developers as AI tools become increasingly widespread.
- Combine traditional programming skills with modern AI tools to optimize productivity.

#### AI, Prototyping, and Pride

- AI helps reduce the time needed to build prototypes and test ideas.
- Even with AI assistance, developers remain responsible for code quality and technical decisions.

### Key Learnings

#### Engineering Mindset & Architecture

- **Scalability-first mindset**: Understand how to design systems from the beginning with scalability in mind, while choosing architectures that meet actual requirements.
- **Prototyping mindset**: Use AI to test ideas quickly, while still performing testing and evaluation before real-world deployment.
- **Engineering fundamentals**: Foundational knowledge of systems, architecture, and problem-solving remains important when using AI tools.

#### The Evolution of Software Development

- Developers do more than write code; they also act as architects and problem solvers, effectively working with AI assistants.
- AI can support repetitive tasks, allowing developers to spend more time on complex technical challenges.
- Staying updated with cloud computing and AI trends is important to avoid falling behind technological developments.

### Practical Applications

- **Integrating AI into workflows**: Use AI tools to support coding, testing, and faster prototype development.
- **Optimizing system design**: Apply engineering-at-scale principles to building and managing individual or team projects.
- **Improving self-learning skills**: Proactively expand knowledge of cloud computing and AI through programs such as First Cloud AI Journey.

### Event Experience

Following the [Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner: Navigating the Future of Cloud & AI in Vietnam](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jWjOIfMA9qc) was a valuable experience that provided deep strategic insights into technology. Some notable experiences included:

#### Learning from Leading Experts

- Gained a better understanding of the long-term vision for cloud computing and AI in Vietnam and global trends.
- Learned practical lessons about developing large-scale systems from industry pioneers.

#### A New Perspective on Modern Developers

- Developed a clearer understanding of the "renaissance developer" concept, in which AI helps automate repetitive tasks so developers can focus on creative thinking and solving core problems.

#### Lessons Learned

- AI tools are powerful enablers that accelerate development, but human expertise and engineering thinking remain decisive.
- Staying updated with cloud computing and AI trends is key to career development in the technology industry.

> Overall, the fireside chat not only provided insights into cloud computing and AI but also offered strong inspiration for developing an engineering mindset and pursuing personal growth in software engineering.
