# Technology Events & Community Workshops

During the 12-week internship in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I actively participated in technology workshops and cloud conferences organized by AWS and community partners. These events offered valuable opportunities to learn directly from leading AWS Principal Architects, network with industry professionals, and stay at the forefront of modern cloud innovations.

Below are the detailed reports for the two major events I attended:

---

### [Event 1: GenAI-powered App & Database Modernization Workshop](4.1-event1/)
* **Event Name:** GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization Workshop  
* **Date & Time:** August 13, 2025 | 09:00 - 17:00  
* **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
* **Role:** Attendee  
* **Core Topics:** Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Event-Driven Architecture, Microservices decomposition, and leveraging Amazon Q Developer to automate legacy code modernizations.

---

### [Event 2: AWS Community Day Vietnam - Accelerating Cloud & Generative AI](4.2-event2/)
* **Event Name:** AWS Community Day Vietnam 2025  
* **Date & Time:** September 20, 2025 | 08:30 - 16:30  
* **Location:** Grand Palace Convention Center, Tan Binh District, Ho Chi Minh City  
* **Role:** Technical Attendee & Community Volunteer Support  
* **Core Topics:** Enterprise generative AI with Amazon Bedrock, multi-agent AI architectures, Kubernetes cost optimization on AWS, and zero-trust cloud security governance.
