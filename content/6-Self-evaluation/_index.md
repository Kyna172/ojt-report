# Student Self-Evaluation & Reflection

During my 12-week internship in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program hosted by **Amazon Web Services Vietnam** from **11/08/2025 to 07/11/2025**, I was immersed in a high-intensity professional engineering environment. This experience allowed me to bridge academic theoretical knowledge with enterprise cloud architecture, hands-on infrastructure automation, and team collaboration.

Throughout the program, I took ownership of designing the **IoT Weather Platform Serverless Proposal**, researching and publishing **3 community technical articles**, and producing the **Hybrid VPC Endpoint Hands-on Workshop**. In daily tasks, I maintained a proactive learning attitude, adhered strictly to engineering standards, and sought constructive feedback from mentors.

### Core Strengths Demonstrated
* **Technical Diligence & Hands-on Curiosity:** I dedicated significant after-hours effort to reproducing complex networking setups in my AWS account, ensuring that every technical detail in my reports was verified empirically.
* **Effective Bilingual Communication:** Authored all technical documentation, code comments, and project summaries in clean, idiomatic English and Vietnamese, facilitating accessibility for varied audiences.
* **Community Engagement:** Regularly participated in discussions on the AWS Study Group forum, assisting fellow interns when they encountered VPC routing or IAM permission roadblocks.

---

### Identified Areas for Continuous Improvement
* **Systematic Troubleshooting Under Pressure:** In early weeks, when encountering networking misconfigurations, I occasionally jumped between multiple variables simultaneously. Mentors guided me to follow a disciplined layer-by-layer diagnostic process (OSI model, Security Groups, NACLs, Route Tables).
* **Time Management for Deep Work:** Balancing in-depth technical research with scheduled sprint deliverables was challenging at times. I plan to adopt stricter timeboxing techniques in future projects.
* **Public Speaking & Live Presentation:** While comfortable presenting technical slides to small cohort groups, presenting before larger executive audiences remains an area I am committed to practicing actively.
